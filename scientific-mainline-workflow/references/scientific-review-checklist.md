# Scientific Review Checklist

Use this checklist for independent review of a mutable candidate. Select only
the sections relevant to the claim, but do not skip a relevant burden because
the artifact is primarily prose.

## Identity and provenance

- Is the base commit current and correctly pinned?
- Does the review identify the exact candidate paths and hashes it covers?
- Are all frozen source files and hashes identified?
- Does the candidate preserve frozen theorem identities?
- Does it avoid modifying a frozen aggregate or source-bound file merely to
  advertise the new result?
- Are declared inputs separated from derived relations?
- Is execution-only engineering clearly separated from scientific identity?
- Is analogous or deprecated material clearly non-authoritative?

## Types and ontology

- Is every variable's mathematical and physical type explicit?
- Are ontic state, derived coordinates, shadows, records, and statistical laws
  kept distinct?
- Are added fields or modes already licensed, or does the candidate silently
  add ontology?
- Are quantum, classical, PDE, reduced, and effective descriptions connected
  by an explicit map or theorem?
- Is exactly one actuality or record-selection mechanism used?

## Mathematics

- Are domains, charts, regularity, boundary conditions, and seam maps explicit?
- Are signs, orientations, symplectic forms, adjoints, and normalizations
  correct?
- Are compactness, boundedness, openness, and finite-dimensionality used
  correctly?
- Does every inverse or projection use the complete relevant space rather than
  a convenient subset?
- Are exact identities recomputed independently?
- Are expected values derived independently from frozen source data rather than
  copied from the constructor's allowlist or table?
- Are approximate claims supplied with prospectively defined bounds?
- Are denominators, lower eigenvalue bounds, and nonempty domains proved
  positive?
- For repeated/generated cases, is there a genuine template or substitution
  theorem whose hypotheses cover every generated row?

## Dynamics and physical completeness

- Is the law local at its declared level?
- Is gauge covariance or invariance proved?
- Are conserved quantities and reciprocal reactions retained?
- Are load-bearing maps, inverses, seam reactions, and source substitutions
  explicit mathematical data rather than prose assertions?
- Are inactive, losing, radiative, exhaust, seam, and archive sectors kept in
  the complete state?
- Does a proposed detector actually capture, amplify, commit, persist, and
  expose a readable record?
- Are record domains robust and nonvacuous?
- Is a live execution left untouched unless the candidate explicitly governs
  its disposition?

## Conditioning and statistics

- Is the complete unconditional alphabet reported first?
- Are misses, ties, nulls, dark events, faults, and unresolved histories
  retained?
- Is any successful-domain conditional law secondary and explicitly labeled?
- Is the preparation measure declared, derived, or selected by a return
  theorem?
- Are source weights absent from detector gains, thresholds, and fault
  handling?
- Are asymptotic convergence and finite-run error kept distinct?

## Bell and no-signalling

- Is the operational algebra prospectively fixed?
- Are admitted observables gauge-invariant and physically implementable?
- Does the no-signalling statement cover all admitted local diagnostics,
  including reaction and faults?
- Is any excluded observable physically screened, gauge variant, outside
  support, or otherwise excluded for a theorem-backed reason?
- Are measurement independence, future-setting independence, and
  setting-independent sampling protected?
- Is the Bell statistic unconditional or governed by a loophole-safe
  inequality?
- Does the violation remain positive after all error and null-event charges?
- Is the exact Bell-assumption failure stated without using vague
  "nonlocality" language?

## Claim discipline

- Is this an exact theorem, conditional theorem, witness, obstruction,
  regression, or selected-realization result?
- Does the inference stay within the proof's domain and horizon?
- Is hosting distinguished from emergence?
- Is finite operational closure distinguished from relativistic or spacelike
  completion?
- Is a selected realization failure kept distinct from a family no-go?
- Are stronger physical provenance, empirical calibration, and axiom
  minimization left as separate questions?
- Is the proof burden proportional to the active program stage rather than
  drifting into unnecessary ultra-deep certification?
- Does the artifact close the active load-bearing question without silently
  promoting nonblocking polish into a pass condition?

## Controls and validation

- Does every decision predicate have at least one genuine semantic mutation?
- Can mutations detect omitted or duplicate objects, sign reversals,
  nonpositive bounds, incomplete reactions, invalid support transport, and
  false dependency expansion when those predicates are in scope?
- Are representation-only changes accepted?
- Are matched negative controls scientifically relevant?
- Can the validator catch missing coordinates, wrong signs, omitted faults,
  changed supports, postselection, and target-table injection?
- Are exact scalar types serialized deterministically?
- Does the validator avoid circular agreement with the builder by recomputing
  decisive expectations through an independent route?
- Are local checks supplemented by the inherited theorem-spine validators?
- Are validator and mutation counts described as process evidence rather than
  theorem strength?

## Review disposition

Return exactly one:

- `ACCEPT` — every declared predicate is positively satisfied;
- `BLOCK` — list the smallest concrete corrections;
- `FAIL SELECTED REALIZATION` — the reviewed candidate cannot meet its declared
  gate without a new identity;
- `CLASS-LEVEL OBSTRUCTION` — only with a theorem covering the declared class.

State surviving positive lemmas even when the overall candidate fails.

Bind the disposition to the reviewed base commit and candidate artifact set.
If a blocking repair changes any scientific byte, inspect the revised artifact
and issue a new disposition rather than carrying forward the old acceptance.
