## Scope and coverage

Apply this workflow when the task concerns a complete paper, thesis, report or comparably long argument. Infer routine audience and output choices from the task. Establish exactly which file or assembled sources constitute the document. Distinguish its own appendices from a separately published supplement used as authority. Do not silently omit difficult appendices or count rewriting the main narrative as rewriting the whole paper.

Freeze the authoritative source and the accepted starting draft. Inventory the abstract, main sections and subsections, theorem/proposition statements and proofs, tables, figure captions, appendices, notes and bibliography. Retain necessary source figures and attribution. Record an explicit disposition for each component: rewritten, retained after review, consolidated into a named destination, or outside the authorized scope. An unchanged equation or reference entry can be appropriate; an unexamined component cannot count as reviewed.

Keep a private coverage table:

| Component | Source identity | Candidate location | Editorial disposition | Comprehension/prose coverage | Fidelity/integrity coverage | Open issues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Section or appendix | File/version and heading | Heading or anchor | Actual treatment | Reviewer and frozen candidate | Reviewer/check and frozen candidate | IDs or none |

Track coverage at a useful substantive level, usually subsections and each major formal result. Do not manufacture a row for every sentence. Separate coverage from acceptance: reading a section is not a pass, and a count of completed rows does not establish a coherent whole.

## Document argument and dependencies

State the governing question and contribution hierarchy before assigning local edits. Map the job of each section and its incoming prerequisites and outgoing result. Distinguish the narrative sequence from the logical proof dependencies: an overview can precede a derivation, but must explain the terms needed to interpret its claim.

Build a compact register of models, apparatuses, parameter regimes, interventions and observation families. For each, record its initial law, dynamics, controls, measured quantities, exact or approximate status and evidence. Shared variables or notation do not transfer a theorem to another construction. Keep the scope attached to a result each time it appears, including in summaries, captions and numerical tables.

Track consequential notation across the document. A reused symbol may be legitimate in a clearly bounded appendix, but its local meaning must be reset before use. Distinguish one component from the whole system, local from integrated quantities, nominal parameters from observed values, and a normalized descriptor from the underlying physical variables. Trace the actual observation or extraction map when later sections admit more general inputs than an earlier ideal example. If the source supplies only a restricted map and its hypotheses, state that conditional scope; do not invent a general reduction. Check normalization and resource units when a result is reused at a larger scale.

Give each central claim a source and a dependency route. Check that the abstract and conclusion promise what the body establishes; that formal statements include their necessary premises; that proof explanations use those premises; and that discussion and examples stay within the resulting domain. A proof sketch should explain why its steps lead to the result, not merely name the files containing them. Keep full technical support accessible without making the reader follow a circular chain of references.

## Section writing and integration

Apply the core skill to the whole argument and then to each section. Match exposition to its role: an introduction motivates, a model section makes objects and assumptions usable, a result section explains what is established and why, and an appendix supplies technical support. Do not force the introduction's historical opening or level of detail on every section.

When work is divided among writers, provide the same source and accepted document contract, assign nonoverlapping output files and stable section interfaces, and keep one integrator responsible for the complete draft. Writers should read enough surrounding material to preserve dependencies. They must not silently create incompatible definitions, revise shared equations independently, or use section-local approval as a complete-paper verdict.

Assemble the full text before declaring a candidate. Then read each join and trace the central argument from beginning to end. Remove duplicate introductions, abrupt returns to already established definitions, promises with no later answer, and conclusions that add unsupported results. Keep repeated material when it adds a distinct layer: overview, physical explanation, formal derivation and implementation can all be useful. Make the added role apparent.

Consolidate repeated derivations only after checking equivalence and recording their destination. An appendix should provide its local setup and point to an authoritative derivation rather than repeat a second drifting version. If an earlier appendix relies on later mechanics, give the minimum usable setup before the derivation and identify the later calculation precisely. Reordering appendices is optional; repairing the dependency is mandatory.

Retain accepted prose unless the assembled document exposes a concrete need to change it. If a previously accepted introduction already explains an example, let the body deepen that example rather than replay it at the same level. Reconcile the roadmap with the actual section roles and preserve distinctions introduced in the body when revising the abstract or conclusion.

## Scientific and document integrity

Use mechanical checks for omissions and broken references, followed by semantic review. Compare equations, labels, numerical claims, tables, citations, anchors and figure references against the source. Prefer a structured document parser when available. Treat differences as prompts to inspect, not automatic errors: a valid consolidation may remove a repeated formula, while unchanged formulas may acquire a false meaning through altered prose.

For each changed or relocated formal result, inspect its definitions, hypotheses, quantifiers, constants, proof dependencies and interpretation together. Check every occurrence of a changed term or convention. Separate a general theorem from its evaluated specialization: the number of stages, outcomes or dimensions and the constants of one example need not be uniform over the general family. Preserve the distinction between a fixed observation horizon and a construction-dependent duration, a sufficient resource choice and a universal requirement, a weighted bound and a conditional guarantee, and a nominal calculation and a finite or experimental certificate.

Check cross-references after assembly. Internal references must reach the intended claim or definition, and external links must identify the correct source version where versions matter. A pointer to a file does not explain a premise needed at the present sentence. Keep bibliography identities and author attributions attached to supported claims; do not invent missing citation details. Record any source ambiguity that editorial work cannot resolve.

Review captions and tables as part of the argument. Preserve their scientific data, but revise opaque headers, compressed prose and ambiguous cross-references when needed; byte-identical tables are not evidence of editorial review. State whether a plot is a schematic, analytic evaluation, simulation or measurement, and whether its symbols and conventions agree with the text. Explain any necessary translation when a preserved figure uses different notation, and identify the parameters behind printed numerical values. Check that an illustrative threshold or label follows the stated convention at every stage. Do not imply that numerical evaluation or a software check proves a stronger analytic or empirical result.

Validate the final rendered artifact as well as the editable text. Inspect every page for missing equations or figures, clipped mathematics, broken tables, unreadable captions, and heading/page-flow problems. Correct rendering defects without silently changing scientific content. A successful build is not a visual review.

## Fresh complete-document reviews

Freeze the assembled candidate and identify it unambiguously. Keep reviews of different versions separate. Use the independent-review protocol; give fresh reviewers the artifact and audience, and the authoritative source only when needed for fidelity. Do not give them author ledgers, prior diagnoses, desired conclusions or instructions to confirm fixes.

Require these complementary readings:

1. **Continuous reader/prose review.** Read the complete document in order, including appendices and captions. Describe the reconstructed contribution and argument, then identify passages that lose continuity, motivation or necessary context. Check whether length is earned by distinct explanatory or technical work. Review flow at ordinary reading speed rather than only answering a questionnaire.
2. **Scientific fidelity review.** Compare the complete candidate with its authority, including formal statements, proofs, numerical tables, appendices and references. Preserve all substantive material or explicitly justify its consolidation. For a very long document, disjoint specialist passes may cover this comparison, but an integration review must check shared definitions, model boundaries and reused results across those partitions.
3. **Long-range consistency review.** Trace claims and terminology across abstract, introduction, model, results, discussion, conclusion and appendices. Verify that scope does not drift and that local improvements have not created duplication, inconsistent notation or broken dependencies. This is a distinct question even if a full reader also checks it.

One reviewer can supply more than one reading only when the coverage and reasoning are explicit. Do not label author self-review as independent. If context or tool limits prevent a complete reading, state the reviewed spans and arrange the missing coverage. An excerpt review, spot check, outline reconstruction or aggregate of local approvals is insufficient evidence for full-paper convergence.

Ask for exact passages, reader effects or source evidence, severity, and a distinction between necessary repairs and optional alternatives. Do not set a quota for findings or request a clean verdict. A reviewer may be wrong; adjudicate its claim using the candidate and source, recording the reason rather than automatically accepting or dismissing it.

## Repair loops and completion

Repair material findings in coherent batches. For each, identify whether the cause is local prose, a missing premise, section order, a repeated claim, notation drift or a problem in the skill's process. Update the general skill instruction when the workflow itself allowed the failure. Keep manuscript-specific diagnoses and answer keys outside the reusable package.

Distinguish an editorial omission from an unresolved source specification. Calling an operation “declared” or a domain “qualified” does not supply a missing definition. Search the relevant authority; if it only assumes the object, make the additional premise precise, including its input, output, required properties and coverage of exceptional cases, and record the exact remaining scientific decision separately. Do not invent an extraction rule, mechanism or proof to obtain a clean verdict. Report editorial closure and unresolved source questions as separate statuses rather than claiming that more polished wording has completed the science.

After repair, inspect the changed passage, its surrounding section and every dependent occurrence elsewhere. Repeat the affected technical checks on the new candidate. Then conduct a fresh complete-document reading without the preceding issue list. A locally improved paragraph may create a new global repetition or mismatch; regression checks alone will miss that.

Do not use a fixed number of loops as a quality certificate. Continue while material reader, prose, fidelity or explicitly requested defects remain. Reassess the approach if two repair batches do not improve the same failure. Stop when the complete argument is coherent, every component has been covered, source-critical claims and document integrity pass, and remaining changes are optional preferences with no identified material benefit. Report the exact scope, versions and nature of the reviews. Convergence is evidence about this document; it is not a guarantee that the skill will perfect every paper.

For skill development, forward-use the revised instructions on the actual task through a fresh writer or editor. If claiming broader transfer, also use a separate multi-section technical document, with raw task inputs and no supplied diagnosis, and independently inspect the result. An introduction-only transfer case does not exercise full-document convergence.
