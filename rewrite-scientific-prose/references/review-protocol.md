## Preparation

Freeze source and candidate, record their versions or checksums, and finish writes before a reviewer reads them. Keep author intentions, claim ledgers, previous reviews and expected answers out of a fresh comprehension review. Use independent readers when available; do not claim independence for repeated self-review.

Separate a targeted regression check from a fresh review. The first verifies that a known repair worked; the second looks for any material problem in the whole candidate. A fresh agent receives only the artifact, audience and neutral task instructions, plus the source for fidelity review. Do not pass conversation history containing the diagnosis, a desired verdict, or a list of fixes to confirm. Keep earlier review artifacts out of its assigned inputs.

## Comprehension

### Isolated opening review

For an introduction, give a fresh reader only its opening paragraphs and the audience description, without the abstract, later explanation, glossary, source or earlier reviews. Use a natural rhetorical boundary rather than a rigid word count. Assess scientific orientation before technical comprehension; correctly reconstructing the apparatus does not establish that the passage functions as an introduction. Ask the reader to explain, in order:

- What recognizable scientific question or phenomenon the opening starts from, and why it makes the work worth reading.
- What the relevant background or historical development establishes, and how the paper's narrower question follows. Distinguish field context from project history and a developing argument from a list of citations.
- Whether the proposed machinery enters after the reader has a reason to examine it, and whether the transition is substantive rather than a generic prefatory sentence.
- What the central object is and what is being done to it.
- Whether the physical or mathematical setup is established before the first specialized example, and whether the reader can sketch its objects and relationships.
- What each consequential preparation, state, label or axis means when first encountered, including the reference frame and relationships among the coordinates or labels.
- What happens in the main example, what changes, and why that creates the stated problem.
- Whether an alternative changes an earlier step or appends a new one; reconstruct both sequences from the prose itself.
- What kind of answer the paper proposes and why the next paragraph follows.
- Which contribution is principal, how the other results depend on or apply it, and whether that hierarchy appears before detailed machinery.

For each answer, separate explicit support, reasonable inference and knowledge imported from elsewhere. Subject familiarity must not repair a missing explanation that the target reader needs here. Flag dense branching, abrupt shifts into formal inventory, and definitions that add opaque terms. Do not demand every later mechanism or proof in the opening, or a historical preamble in a genre that does not need one. Where history or earlier work supplies motivation, the fidelity review must verify those claims and any asserted gap.

For each new construction in the full passage, check whether its purpose or the difficulty requiring it is established before its name and technical specification. A correct definition does not compensate for an unmotivated construction.

Treat a user's specific report of confusion as evidence to investigate even after a previous pass. Acceptance requires a local explanation of that concept and a reader who can use it in the example; a later definition or an expert's fluent paraphrase is insufficient. When the opening is the requested priority, finish this gate before rewriting the complete excerpt. After integration, check its immediate transitions and consistency again.

### Full passage

Give the reviewer only the candidate and intended audience. Ask them to answer from the passage and quote evidence, allowing "not explained":

1. What problem does the passage address?
2. What are the actors or objects, and what happens in order?
3. What produces variation, randomness or uncertainty?
4. What changes, and what information or quantity is preserved?
5. What is the principal result and its domain?
6. Which ingredients are assumptions or design choices?
7. What exactly is compared, and which inputs are shared?
8. What does the main control change and keep fixed?
9. What do important numbers count or measure, and why do they matter?
10. What remains unproved or physically unidentified?
11. What is new relative to the background described, and which supplied ingredients carry part of the explanation?
12. Which paragraphs are understandable but unnecessary at their current location, and what would be lost by moving them?

Mark inapplicable questions with reasons. Request critical/major/minor findings with locations and plausible interpretations. Distinguish understanding supplied by the text from background knowledge or conjecture. Do not ask for a numerical readability score or reward short sentences.

Fail this gate if a necessary answer requires guessing, two reasonable interpretations change the claim, or the central process cannot be followed.

Pay particular attention to transitions between representations: a reader who can repeat the desired outcome may still not understand how the stated operation produces it. Ask which variables are acted on and why that action has the claimed effect. Do not demand an entire proof in an introduction; require only the bridge needed to understand the central mechanism. Check whether an impressive number has its reference value and whether formal detail has displaced the explanatory hierarchy.

Check the premises of each reduction. Ask whether a local descriptor has silently become a state for the whole system, whether a restricted preparation has become generic, and whether a weighted statement has become a guarantee for every conditional case. Distinguish the system's physical properties, the operation's outputs and the reduced quantities compared. The reader need not reconstruct a proof, but must not need to invent the premise that makes the explanation valid.

Audit placement separately from definition. State the role of each substantial technical paragraph in the current argument. If removing or relocating it leaves the argument complete and makes the contribution easier to follow, recommend that move with a destination. Inspect repeated overview/example/derivation passes: each return must add understanding. Do not reward maximal compression or remove assumptions that determine the interpretation of a nearby claim.

## Prose flow

Perform a separate pass at ordinary reading speed, using only the candidate and audience. Do not infer fluency from a successful comprehension questionnaire or a checklist of definitions. Read whole paragraphs and the joins between them, not a set of isolated sentences.

Ask the reviewer to identify passages where they must mentally translate stiff wording, infer a purpose or relation that has not been established, or restart because the topic has changed abruptly. Check in particular:

- Whether each sentence develops an active subject or question and gives the next sentence a useful starting point.
- Whether an objective, benchmark, requirement, intervention or comparison has its necessary context before a shorthand label stands for it.
- Whether the prose tells what happens or repeatedly announces an editorial task.
- Whether definitions are integrated into the explanation or presented as disconnected dictionary entries.
- Whether subordination, contrast and qualification reveal the hierarchy of the claims, with varied but unforced rhythm.
- Whether transitions convey a real connection rather than adding generic connective words.
- Whether qualification-heavy paragraphs establish the purpose of their conditions before long lists, and whether repeated scope or matching statements could be consolidated without losing distinct requirements.
- Whether defined terminology and notation still impose avoidable translation, and whether a functional description or motivated convention would reduce that burden.

Require exact passages and explanations of the reading effect. Do not impose a ban on particular words, demand ornamental language, reward length, invent causal links, or treat every technical term as a defect. Distinguish a recurring failure from an optional alternate phrasing.

When naturalness or flow is the user's explicit concern, keep substantiated failures open even if comprehension and fidelity pass. After repair, compare versions only in a separately labeled comparative pass; a fresh first-reading review should not receive the earlier diagnosis or preferred sentences.

## Fidelity

Give a reviewer source and candidate without the editor's intended verdict. Check assumptions, scope, quantifiers, laws, interventions, result types, numbers, equations, units, conservation/information claims, matched observations, excluded observations, citations, model/version boundaries, and relocation/omission. Check added historical context against sources, including chronology, what earlier work established, and whether the claimed gap is general or only the paper's chosen construction problem.

Trace each reduced descriptor to the preparation or mathematical restriction that validates it. For estimates, check the metric and weighting, any normalization denominator, the fixed domain, and which resources may depend on accuracy. Do not infer pointwise or rare-event conditional accuracy from aggregate control. Distinguish exact reference statements from finite approximations and empirical identifications. Check these semantic dependencies even when the mathematical tokens are unchanged.

Distinguish unsupported changes from harmless rephrasing or justified consolidation. Require source locations for material findings. Reconcile the working ledger afterward.

## Complete documents

For a paper, thesis or report, apply [the full-document workflow](full-document-workflow.md). Freeze and review the assembled text, including its appendices, captions, tables and references. Keep a coverage record, conduct a continuous full reading, and check long-range claims, notation, model boundaries and dependencies separately. Partitioned fidelity reviews require a final integration review. An unchanged or accepted introduction does not certify the remaining document, and section-level passes do not establish global coherence.

Require reviewers to identify what they actually read and which frozen candidate they assessed. After material integration repairs, renew affected fidelity and the complete-document reading. Do not call an outline review or a sample of pages a full review. The convergence decision belongs to the assembled deliverable and its full coverage, not the number of agents or loops.

## Closure

Repair critical/major findings and specifically requested language defects, or identify the missing source premise. Repeat affected review after material changes. Never downgrade a defect just to pass. Close comprehension, prose flow and fidelity separately; success on one does not imply success on the others. Adjudicate proposed defects against the text and source; reviewers can overread, request unnecessary detail or disagree about equally natural phrasings.

Resolve every requested catalogue item with evidence or mark it inapplicable with a reason. Keep unresolved items visible. A clean scoped review does not prove universal readability or scientific truth.

Verify quotations and material findings against the frozen candidate. Separate the reader's inferences from statements actually present. If drafts were mixed, repeat the affected review on the intended version before declaring closure.

When local repairs stabilize, freeze the complete candidate and run a fresh whole-output review. Do not feed it the previous issue list. Require concrete passages and reader effects for new concerns, without a quota or a requested clean verdict. If material changes follow, check their surrounding paragraphs and the argument's hierarchy again; compression can remove a premise, while qualification can create duplication.

Convergence requires a clear hierarchy appropriate to the genre, an intelligible mechanism at the requested level, connected prose, source-faithful claims, and no open material or specifically requested defect. Record the evidence and any residual stylistic preferences. Stop when further edits are optional alternatives with no identified reader or fidelity benefit. Do not equate convergence with universal perfection, checklist completion or agreement among reviewers.

## Forward use during skill development

When asked to build or improve an editing skill:

- Give a fresh agent the current skill path, raw source and a normal editing request.
- Include the draft being edited when it is the actual task input, but do not provide expected prose, diagnosis, author ledgers or alternative candidates.
- Review the produced text through all three gates.
- Revise the general instruction that allowed a failure; rerun from raw source in a fresh work directory.
- Use an unrelated technical passage before claiming transfer beyond the main example.
- Preserve prompts, skill versions and reviews outside the distributable skill.
- Keep private manuscript content out of reusable examples unless explicitly needed and authorized.
