# Diagnostic catalogue

Use these checks for a reader unfamiliar with the author's project. Preserve legitimate technical content; repair the explanatory failure. These patterns do not impose a physics-paper template on other genres.

## Architecture and reader orientation

| ID | Failure to detect | Repair | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W01 | The opening starts the author's construction without establishing the scientific question, or several questions compete without priority. | Orient the reader to the broader problem and its significance, then establish the paper's narrower question and subordinate applications. | A reader explains why the question matters and identifies one principal task before learning the proposed machinery. |
| W02 | Results are listed without priority, or the principal achievement appears only after its machinery. | State the main achievement early; relate dependent results, approximations and applications to it. Explain what prior work supplies and what is added on the same axis. | Before the detailed construction, a reader identifies the principal result, its assumptions and the role of the other results. |
| W03 | Objects are named before a usable picture exists, or setup is mistaken for scientific orientation. | Establish the reason for examining the situation, then its objects, relationships, operation and reference frame before a labeled instance. | A reader understands why this setup appears and describes its arrangement and coordinate meanings before the example uses them. |
| W04 | A construction precedes its motivation, or motivation is supplied only by a formulaic task announcement. | Establish the need through the preceding explanation, then introduce the construction and the necessary specification. | Each new object has an intelligible reason for appearing, and the paragraph develops the argument without a repeated purpose label. |
| W05 | A motivating example is abandoned, overloaded with ambiguous branches or used to support an inference whose premise is missing. | Establish its initial condition, follow one main sequence and explain what supports the conclusion. Add necessary comparisons, specifying whether an alternative replaces or appends a step. Carry the example through the proposed explanation. | A reader follows each event order and can explain why the example creates the stated problem, without supplying an unstated premise. |
| W06 | Physical, mathematical and statistical levels blur. | Mark full state, reduced description, ensemble, observation and theorem. | A reader classifies the main statements correctly. |
| W07 | The abstract inventories the project. | Rank problem, mechanism, principal result and scope. | The abstract has a recoverable argument. |
| W43 | A technically understandable paragraph is misplaced, delaying the result or turning an introduction into methods. | Assign each paragraph a current explanatory job; remove or relocate detail whose absence does not break the argument. Keep necessary interpretive assumptions locally and record the destination of moved detail. | A reader reaches the contribution before its recipe; each retained technical paragraph is needed there, rather than merely accurate or well defined. |

## Claims and scientific interpretation

| ID | Failure to detect | Repair | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W08 | A protocol requirement is universalized. | Bound the opening claim to the intended task. | The sentence promises no more than the source. |
| W09 | Benchmark, assumption and derived result blur, including mismatched outcome sets or protocols. | Identify the comparison before its predictions, then make the return to the proposed model and its conditions explicit. Explain additional outcomes and any conditioning needed for agreement. | The status and domain of each predictive claim are clear at first use; announcing a comparison alone or using an unexplained "target" label is insufficient. |
| W10 | A functional label substitutes for a mechanism. | Explain what variable changes through what interaction. | A reader follows one complete causal or logical chain. |
| W11 | Randomness or variation is asserted but not traced. | Separate sampling, evolution and observation where applicable. | A reader explains what is random and how frequencies arise. |
| W12 | An assumed structure is confused with derived behavior, or qualifications obscure what was achieved. | Explain what inputs prescribe and what the construction establishes using them. Connect that achievement to the stated difficulty. | A reader can separate supplied structure from demonstrated behavior and explain the positive contribution. |
| W13 | Exact, approximate and physically identified results blur. | Give each result its domain and evidence status. | A reader distinguishes equality, approximation and realization. |
| W14 | A match or equivalence lacks an operational definition. | Name compared objects, observations or metric, and common inputs. | A reader says what agrees and what is outside the claim. |
| W15 | A central identifying assumption hides in a modifier. | Explain it plainly beside the headline result. | The result can be interpreted without a later correction. |
| W16 | A control lacks an explicit intervention. | Name changed/fixed variables, laws and independence relationships. | A reader describes the comparison experiment or calculation. |
| W17 | Completeness counts lack a defined unit. | Explain the alphabet, protocol or enumeration. | A reader reconstructs the count and its limits. |
| W18 | Precise values or coordinate labels lack context, or a convention imposes an unexplained conversion. | Establish the reference arrangement, quantities, units, normalization, baseline and significance; motivate a nonstandard convention or translate it consistently into simpler notation. | A reader knows what labels refer to, why a convention is used and what the value or inequality establishes. |
| W19 | Readiness, success, nulls, faults and exclusions blur. | Distinguish mechanisms and statistical treatment. | A reader traces each category through the calculation. |
| W20 | Robustness/resource conditions lack an explained role. | Explain avoided degeneracies and the achievement that remains. | Sufficient resources, necessary bounds and practicality remain distinct. |
| W44 | A reduced descriptor is presented without the premises that make it describe the stated object. | Trace the map from full variables through restriction, normalization, projection or averaging. State needed uniformity, preparation, factorization or coherence assumptions and what information is lost. | A reader distinguishes a local descriptor from a common state, and a chosen preparation from an arbitrary configuration; the source supports the reduction. |
| W45 | An aggregate or weighted guarantee becomes a claim about every normalized case, or an approximation hides its quantifier order. | Preserve the metric, conditioning and probability denominators. State what is fixed and which resources may depend on the requested accuracy; keep ideal equality separate from finite approximation. | The revised guarantee is no stronger than the source, including on rare cases and in the order of limits or choices. |

## Vocabulary and relations

| ID | Failure to detect | Repair | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W21 | Noun stacks compress unstated relationships. | Expand the relation before reusing a short label. | No private glossary is needed for the central claim. |
| W22 | Familiar words acquire unexplained specialized meanings, or a defined private term still makes the reader translate. | Disambiguate consequential terms at first use; establish their function and use necessary names consistently. Prefer a direct description when the metaphor or abbreviation adds burden. | Meaning and role follow from local context; the term earns its repeated use. |
| W23 | Definitions are missing, circular or equally opaque. | State referent, role and relevant operation or observable consequence; explain labels and conventions needed to use it. | A reader can apply the definition in the example without silently supplying it from expertise. |
| W24 | Independence or conditioning lacks its variables. | State what is independent of what and under which law. | Coordinate freedom, statistical independence and separate specification stay distinct. |
| W25 | Supplied/stated/chosen hides kinds of premise. | Separate constitutive hypotheses, design, calibration, preparation and analytic conditions. | A reader knows what would justify each input. |
| W26 | Related terms lack a hierarchy, or a physical property is conflated with a recorded result. | Map full state, physical property, reduced variable, ensemble, detector/algorithm output and summary; explain the measurement or computation connecting them. | Terms stay distinct but connected, without implying that every output directly reveals a pre-existing value. |
| W27 | Relational verbs and nouns omit a necessary participant, objective or comparison. | Name the relevant relation, such as what is matched, who seeks an outcome, what a requirement serves or what an intervention changes. | The claim is intelligible from active context without a reader inventing the missing relation. |
| W28 | Abstract subjects obscure agency or substitute an editorial label for the subject matter. | Give actions to systems and claims to arguments; state the author's purpose explicitly when needed, otherwise describe the event or relation directly. | A reader distinguishes acting, intending and describing without mentally translating a label or supplying an unstated actor. |
| W29 | Slashes or invented compounds hide relationships. | Write the conjunction, alternative or dependency. | The relation is clear without decoding punctuation. |

## Sentences

| ID | Failure to detect | Repair | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W30 | Pronouns or abbreviated nouns lack referents. | Name the actor or quantity where needed. | Consequential referents are locally recoverable. |
| W31 | An example switches to internal coordinates without the map or retained quantities needed to interpret their change. | Connect the coordinate operation to the reconstructed physical variables and invariants, or defer the partial calculation and explain the physical operation. | The mechanism remains intelligible without mistaking a coordinate exchange for a different physical transfer or inventing a missing invariant. |
| W32 | Comparisons omit counterparts or force unlike arguments under one verb. | Specify comparison axes and separate claims when needed. | No missing argument must be guessed. |
| W33 | Sentences are individually clear but read as disconnected announcements or definitions. | Develop an active subject or question across sentences; combine dependencies and vary structure according to explanation, consequence and qualification. | A continuous-reading pass finds a clear progression within and between paragraphs, without repeated restarts or empty connective glue. |
| W34 | Parallel lists mix logical categories. | Separate properties, mechanisms, evidence and guarantees. | Each list has an organizing principle. |

## Rhetoric and document design

| ID | Failure to detect | Repair | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W35 | Missing or misplaced background leaves the reader without context; a literature list or project history substitutes for the field's question. | Use relevant historical developments and earlier findings to motivate the question before the construction; put detailed comparisons where their objects are understood. Consolidate displaced repetitions. | The opening explains what is known, why this question arises and how the paper fits, without invented gaps or a list of names standing in for an argument. |
| W36 | Prior work is clearer than the contribution. | Explain the new work concretely on the same relevant axis. | A reader can distinguish contributions without decoding labels. |
| W37 | Anticipated objections become the organizing voice. | Give a positive account with limits attached to their claims. | Scope is explicit without dominating the narrative. |
| W38 | Repetition adds length or resets an explanation already underway. | Make each return add intuition, mechanism, result or limitation; integrate definitions instead of repeating them as purpose statements. | Repeated content advances the same developing thought without forcing the reader to start over. |
| W39 | Tables introduce prerequisites instead of summarizing. | Teach relationships first; define symbols or move the inventory. | Each retained table is usable on first encounter. |
| W40 | Classification structures or specification inventories displace the introduction's explanatory purpose. | Establish context, motivation and contribution; retain only formal detail needed at that point. | The opening brings the reader into the scientific problem before developing the construction. |
| W41 | Cross-references defer understanding needed now. | Give the minimum local explanation and refer for derivation. | The paragraph is intelligible before following a reference. |
| W42 | Dense exposition offers no consolidation. | Use a complete example, useful schematic or short synthesis. | A reader reconstructs the chain from the passage and can follow the opening before reading ahead. |
| W46 | Necessary qualifications accumulate as lists, repeated matching statements or appended caveats. | Rebuild the paragraph around its main claim and the purpose of the conditions; consolidate repetitions while preserving genuinely different premises. | A continuous reading follows motivation, claim and qualification without restarts; the repair improves flow without weakening scope. |

## Whole-document failures

| ID | Failure to detect | Repair | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W47 | Local section approvals or an accepted opening are presented as full-paper convergence while appendices, captions or other components are unexamined. | Establish complete coverage, assemble the candidate and conduct fresh continuous and long-range reviews of that exact text. | Every component has a supported disposition and the final assembled argument has been read; partial reviews remain explicitly partial. |
| W48 | Individually improved sections repeat the same explanation, break prerequisites or create circular appendix dependencies. | Map each section's role and interfaces, integrate the prose, consolidate equivalent derivations into named destinations and provide needed local setup. | A beginning-to-end reader follows the progression without redundant restarts or having to chase an unavailable prerequisite. |
| W49 | A term, symbol, normalization or control changes meaning across sections, especially between component/system or nominal/observed descriptions. | Trace uses in a document-wide register, state local scope and repair every affected occurrence and reference together. | Equations, examples, captions and conclusions use compatible meanings; legitimate local reuse is explicit. |
| W50 | A valid result acquires stronger scope in the abstract, discussion, figure or conclusion, or transfers between related constructions. | Trace each summary claim to its actual premises, evidence and model; preserve the boundary every time the result is reused. Check collective references such as “these properties”: one example need not demonstrate every result in the preceding sentence. | A complete-document consistency review finds no promotion of an ideal, conditional, nominal or separately scoped result into a stronger guarantee, or attribution of a general result to an example that does not establish it. |

## Prioritization

Treat wrong scope, unsupported explanations, hidden identifying assumptions and failures of the central mechanism as critical. Treat other failures that prevent the intended reader from following the argument as major. Recurring choppiness, ungrounded rhetorical labels or broken continuity can require repair even when the reader can reconstruct the facts, especially when natural prose is the stated task. Distinguish those observable failures from optional preferences between equally natural phrasings. Severity labels must not excuse an unresolved issue the user specifically asked to fix.

Retain a user's original audit numbering in the working disposition table. W identifiers support diagnosis; keep them out of manuscript prose.

Use the catalogue to discover failures, then review the complete prose without its headings or an expected verdict. A filled table is evidence about specific findings, not a proof that no other concern remains. Do not manufacture issues to fill categories or inflate a preference into a defect.
